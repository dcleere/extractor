1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions apply:

"Battery Pack" means a set of interconnected portable electrochemical cells with a rated energy capacity exceeding 2 kWh, designed to be removable by the end user without the use of tools.

"Placing on the Market" means the first making available of a Battery Pack on the Union market.

"Competent Authority" means the national body designated by a Member State to monitor compliance with this Regulation, coordinated at Union level by the European Battery Safety Authority.

2. Scope

This Regulation applies to Battery Packs placed on the market within the European Union on or after the effective date set out in Section 6, including those incorporated into portable consumer electronics and light electric vehicles.

3. Obligations

Manufacturers shall ensure that every Battery Pack placed on the market carries a label disclosing rated capacity, cell chemistry, and a QR code linking to a digital safety data sheet, in accordance with the technical requirements of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.

Importers shall verify, before placing a Battery Pack on the market, that the manufacturer has carried out the applicable conformity assessment procedure and shall retain supporting documentation for a period of ten years.

Distributors shall notify their Competent Authority without undue delay upon becoming aware that a Battery Pack presents a risk to consumer safety.

4. Exemptions

This Regulation does not apply to Battery Packs that are:

  • incorporated exclusively into medical devices regulated under Regulation (EU) 2017/745;
  • manufactured solely for export outside the European Union and not placed on the Union market;
  • supplied in quantities of fewer than 500 units per year by a single manufacturer for research and development purposes.

5. Penalties

A Competent Authority may impose an administrative fine of up to €500,000 or 2% of the undertaking's total annual turnover, whichever is higher, on any manufacturer, importer, or distributor found to be in material non-compliance with Sections 3 or 4 of this Regulation.

Persistent non-compliance following a first administrative fine may result in suspension of the undertaking's authorization to place Battery Packs on the market in the European Union.

6. Effective Date

This Regulation shall enter into force on 1 January 2027. Undertakings already placing Battery Packs on the market as of the date of publication shall have until 1 July 2027 to achieve full compliance with the labelling requirements of Section 3.
